Import account export

1. Choose the CSV or Excel export from your appraisal software and its as-of date. For Excel, put the account table on the first worksheet. 2. Check the suggested columns and sample rows. 3. Import. Required: account number and physical address. Include owner/business name, DBA, ZIP and tax year when available. The account number can also serve as the property ID. Keep account numbers as text in Excel to preserve leading zeroes. Existing names and reviewed matches are preserved.

Select this for a BPP-only export, including counties with numeric account numbers. Leave it unchecked for an already-configured mixed-property export.

Appraiser activity

Outcomes and daily activity

Product impact export contains aggregate totals only, without appraiser names or account details. Counts show activity in Rendition Pilot, not verified changes in CAD, appraisal value gains, or measured time savings. Analytics begins when tracking is enabled; earlier work is not backfilled.
